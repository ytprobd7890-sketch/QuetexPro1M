    The 1M playbook

    Six setups, one risk model. Every idea is filtered through trend, structure, momentum, VWAP and participation before a position exists — then sized off the ATR stop so no single trade can define the month.

    Risk model
    • 1% risk per trade of live equity. 0.5% when the ATR percentile is above 0.8 or the session is off-hours.
    • Stop is structural, not cosmetic. ATR × 1.5–2.0, placed beyond the invalidation swing — never widened after entry.
    • Reward must be ≥ 2R for trend models, ≥ 1.2R for mean reversion. If the structure cannot offer it, there is no trade.
    • Daily stop: −2R closes the desk for the day. Drawdown is a budget, not an emotion.
    • Concurrent risk cap: 3% total across all open positions; correlated pairs count as one.
    • Move to breakeven at +1R, trail with a 2× ATR band from the extreme. Let the tail pay for the chop.
    • Leverage is an output. Position size comes from the stop distance; leverage is whatever that produces, capped by the account.

    Daily routine
    1. Bias first: mark the 200 EMA and daily VWAP on the higher timeframe. Longs below the 200 EMA need a range-reversion setup, not a breakout.
    2. Mark the last session high/low and the 20-bar range. Those are the only levels that matter for the first hour.
    3. Set alerts at the levels. Do not click mid-range.
    4. Take the first A-setup. Journal it with the screenshot, the R outcome and whether you followed the plan.
    5. Stop at −2R or after three losses. Review, do not revenge.
